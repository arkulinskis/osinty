Code of conduct

The line we don't cross

  1. Open sources only. No hacking, no guessed passwords, no stolen or leaked data.
  2. No pretexting, no impersonation. We read what's public; we don't lie to get the rest.
  3. Observe, never touch. No liking, following, messaging or calling a subject.
  4. A legitimate purpose, every time. We refuse jobs that look like stalking, harassment or revenge.
  5. Proportionate and private. Collect only what the question needs, and keep it safe.

Questions

Will Osinty make me a licensed private investigator?

No. Many places require a licence to investigate for pay, issued by a regulator after experience, training and checks. Osinty teaches the open-source skills and the professional habits; the licence comes from your regulator. Lesson 8.1 explains how to find the rules where you live.

Is any of this illegal?

No. Osinty only teaches lawful, open-source methods: reading what is already public. No hacking, no pretexting, no impersonation, no stolen data. The case files are fictional, and their websites use the reserved .example domain.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use a search engine, you can start. The lessons build up from there, and the gadgets do the fiddly parts for you.

Is it free?

Yes, all of it: lessons, case files, casebook and field kit.

Who can see my casebook?

Only you. Other agents see your display name and XP on the leaderboard, and you can hide even that. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen.
